# Emma & Eric Wedding

## Direction

The site should feel like a joyful garden party drawn by hand: warm, expressive,
and personal without becoming rustic, overly formal, or generically bridal.

Tulips inspired the color relationships in the original references. They are
not the literal theme. Use original mixed botanical forms that feel natural for
Botanica, The Wichita Gardens.

## Palette

- Warm ivory: `#FBF6EE`
- Pale blush: `#FBEDE8`
- Coral: `#F27B76`
- Poppy: `#EF5351`
- Marigold: `#F8B942`
- Tangerine: `#F58A2A`
- Leaf: `#6E982C`
- Dark olive: `#44472F`

Do not introduce a blue background. Ivory is the primary paper tone; pale blush
can separate sections.

## Typography

The final handwriting direction is deliberately undecided. The style board
compares:

1. Borel with Fraunces for a playful garden direction.
2. La Belle Aurore with Cormorant Garamond for a loose romantic direction.
3. Beth Ellen with Fraunces as a stand-in for digitized personal handwriting.

Handwriting is an accent for names and expressive moments. Body copy, forms,
navigation, and logistical details must remain highly readable.

## Content Rules

- Never invent relationship stories, biographies, wedding-party information,
  travel guidance, event logistics, dress codes, FAQs, or registry details.
- During the draft, use explicit content placeholders instead of plausible
  filler copy.
- Unconfirmed content stays `null`, empty, or unpublished in the central
  content model.
- Incomplete sections are visible only while the site status is `draft` and are
  hidden when it becomes `published`.
- Do not publish personal photography until Eric or Emma supplies and approves
  it.
